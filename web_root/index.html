The Club

Montreal, by the Mountain

The Montreal Cigar Club was founded in 2026 by a small circle of collectors who wanted one thing: a room in this city where a serious cigar, a serious pour, and serious music could be enjoyed without hurry.

We source directly — Estelí, the Dominican Republic, and the Canadian domestic market — and rest every stick in Spanish cedar before it reaches a member. The club is bilingual, private, and unapologetically Montréalais: the fleur-de-lis on our crest is not decoration.

Founding Curator

Selection, sourcing, and the vault. Twenty years of collecting, three continents.

Music & Salons

Programs each evening from an analog jazz collection; runs the room.

Committee

Three founding members vet every application. Decisions are unanimous or not at all.
