## 2. Unit Economics: Monthly Salon (20 Guests)

### Revenue:
* **20 Guests × $225 CAD:** **$4,500 CAD**

### Expenses:
* **Venue Rental (4 hours):** ~$800 CAD (e.g. Dreamcatcher Lounge or Old Port Penthouse)
* **Cigars (2 sticks / guest):** 1 × Padrón 1964 ($28) + 1 × E.P. Carrillo Encore ($35) = ~$1,260 CAD
* **Spirits & Pairings (XO Cognac / Aged Rum):** ~$350 CAD *(or $0 with distributor sponsorship)*
* **Artisanal Charcuterie & Bites:** ~$300 CAD
* **Live Vinyl DJ (3 hours):** ~$400 CAD
* **Total Event Cost:** ~$3,110 CAD

### Net Profit per Evening:
* **~$1,400 – $1,800 CAD pure profit**

## 3. Venue Logistics & Smoking Friendly Options

1. **Kahnawake (Mohawk Territory — 15–20 min from Montreal):**
   * **Dreamcatcher Cigar Lounge:** Private rooms, scotch bar, legal year-round indoor smoking under reserve status.
   * **Mirela's Steakhouse & Cigar Lounge:** Full dining + cigar pairing.
2. **Montreal Downtown & Old Port:**
   * **Peerspace / Airbnb Luxe Penthouses:** Private rooftop terraces for open-air luxury tastings.
   * **Old Port Boat Charters:** Summer sunset tasting cruises on the St. Lawrence River.

## 4. Live Entertainment: Vinyl DJ & Acoustic Jazz

* **Soundtrack Profile:** Analog Afro-Cuban jazz, Oscar Peterson Trio, Miles Davis, French Bossa Nova, and downtempo lounge.
* **Live Soloist Pairing:** Live saxophonist or Spanish guitarist playing alongside the DJ during lighting ceremonies.
* **Acoustics:** High-fidelity hardware (B&O Beosound A1 / analog tube warmth).
