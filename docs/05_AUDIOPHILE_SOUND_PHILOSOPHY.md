**Montreal Cigar Club — Acoustic Architecture for Tastings**

## 1. The Acoustic Philosophy
A luxury cigar tasting is a multi-sensory ritual. Compressed, boomy Bluetooth audio degrades the contemplative nature of fine tobacco. The club prioritizes natural midrange fidelity, articulate acoustic double-bass, and airy high-frequency cymbal decay.

## 3. High-Fidelity Setup Techniques
1. **Direct USB-C Lossless Connection:** Plug the Beosound A1 directly into a computer/phone to utilize the internal DAC for uncompressed bit-perfect 24-bit audio (Apple Music Lossless, Tidal, FLAC).
2. **True Wireless Stereo (TWS):** Link two Beosound A1 units in stereo for true Left/Right soundstage separation across outdoor patio salons.
3. **Surface Boundary Coupling:** Placing the speaker flat on a heavy wood or marble table reinforces 55Hz acoustic bass notes naturally without artificial DSP distortion.
