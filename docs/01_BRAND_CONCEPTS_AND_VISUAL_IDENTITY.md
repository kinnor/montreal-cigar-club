# 01 — Brand Concepts & Visual Identity
**Montreal Cigar Club (*Club de Cigare de Montréal*)**

---

## 1. Brand Essence & Philosophy
The Montreal Cigar Club embodies the intersection of Old World European sophistication and contemporary North American metropolitan energy. Rooted in Montreal's Golden Square Mile legacy and rich jazz heritage, the club provides an exclusive sanctuary for refined relaxation, rare vitolas, fine spirits, and elevated conversation.

* **Core Pillars:**
  1. **Mastery:** Curating only world-class, critically acclaimed tobacco blends (94+ rating standard).
  2. **Camaraderie:** An intimate, vetted private circle of connoisseurs, founders, and collectors.
  3. **Discretion:** Absolute privacy, secured humidor vaults, and confidential salon locations.

---

## 2. Visual Identity & Design System

### A. Color Palette
* **Midnight Obsidian (#07080A):** The deep, velvet black foundation representing night salons and private discretion.
* **24K Liquid Gold (#D4AF37 / #F5DF95):** Metallic gold foil accents symbolizing luxury, heritage, and distinction.
* **Smoldering Amber (#E5A93C / #F59E0B):** Warm glowing ember tones representing lit cigars, aged cognac, and intimate lighting.
* **British Racing Emerald (#0B281E):** Traditional luxury tone for vintage heritage badges and member seals.

### B. Typography
* **Primary Header:** *Cinzel* — High-contrast, regal Roman serif for authoritative titles and crest text.
* **Accent & Editorial:** *Cormorant Garamond* — Flowing French italic luxury serif for tasting notes, philosophy, and quotes.
* **Interface & Data:** *Plus Jakarta Sans* / *Inter* — Clean modern sans-serif for telemetry data and interactive UI.
