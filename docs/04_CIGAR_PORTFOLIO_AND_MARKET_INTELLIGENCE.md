## 1. Masterpiece Vitolas

### A. Padrón 1964 Anniversary Series (Principe Maduro — 4.5" x 46)
* **Origin:** Estelí, Nicaragua (Tabacos Cubanica S.A.).
* **Blend:** 100% Nicaraguan Puro aged a minimum of 4 full years.
* **Tasting Notes:** Bittersweet dark cocoa, roasted espresso, cracked black pepper, damp earth, sweet molasses.
* **Smoking Dynamic:** Signature open, effortless draw with dense, velvety white smoke output.

### B. E.P. Carrillo Encore (Majestic — 5 3/8" x 52 / Celestial — 6 1/8" x 50)
* **Origin:** Santiago, Dominican Republic (Tabacalera La Alianza).
* **Accolades:** **96 Points & #1 Cigar of the Year (Cigar Aficionado)**.
* **Blend Secret:** Wrapper leaves aged in palm-bark *tercios* for two years, enabling slow, cool fermentation.
* **Tasting Notes:** Aristocratic sweet cedar, toasted almonds, cinnamon, nutmeg, orange-peel citrus tea, and creamy coffee.

### C. My Father Le Bijou 1922 (Torpedo Box-Pressed — 6 1/8" x 52)
* **Origin:** Estelí, Nicaragua (My Father Cigars).
* **Accolades:** **97 Points & #1 Cigar of the Year**.
* **Blend:** Jet-black Nicaraguan Habano Oscuro wrapper over rich Nicaraguan long-fillers.
* **Tasting Notes:** Dark chocolate, ripe raisins, crushed black pepper, dark roasted espresso.
